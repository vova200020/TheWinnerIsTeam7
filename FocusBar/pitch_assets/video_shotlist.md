# Fallback video: shot list (one continuous take, ~55 s)

Setup: fēnix 7 Pro with the stage build (`stage/FocusBar.prg` + `stage/FocusFace.prg`), iPhone with
**FocusBarLite**, both on a dark table. Phone camera in landscape, both devices in frame, no faces,
good light. FocusBar open on the watch **and** on the phone before you press record (messages only
reach a running app). Demo mode **ON** on the watch (see REPORT.md, item 1 in "Not confirmed").

Timings use the stage timeline measured in the simulator: dip at 30 s, "Take 5?" at ~35 s of session time.

| # | Time | Camera on | What happens | Presenter line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:05 | Watch | The watch face is **green**: "[Focus window] 82" | "My watch says my body is ready to focus right now." |
| 2 | 0:05–0:10 | Watch | Tap the face → quick start "Coding · 25 min · DEEP" → press **START** | "One tap, one press: a 25-minute deep focus block." |
| 3 | 0:10–0:15 | Phone | The Phone tab: Feed / Shorts / Chat get the lock icon | "My phone just locked the distracting apps." |
| 4 | 0:15–0:20 | Phone | Tap **Feed** → "Locked for coding" → **Unlock with watch** | "I want my feed? I have to earn it." |
| 5 | 0:20–0:40 | Both | The watch shows **[Move to unlock]**, target ~90 bpm. Jumping jacks; the ring fills for 15 s | "Raise your heart rate and hold it. Fifteen seconds of movement." |
| 6 | 0:40–0:45 | Both | Watch: "Unlocked". Phone: Feed opens with a "4:59 left" countdown | "Five minutes of scroll, earned." |
| 7 | 0:45–0:50 | Watch | Back in the session: focus dips (amber) → **Take 5?** → End | "When my focus fades, it tells me to take a break." |
| 8 | 0:50–0:58 | Watch | Rate 4/5 → summary: score, **+XP**, award | "Every session builds the habit: XP, awards, a weekly goal." |

Notes
- Step 6 is the part to rehearse first: in the simulator the challenge stopped at "hold 1 s more"
  (see REPORT.md). If it does that on the real watch, record steps 5–6 with the **breathing alternative**
  (press START on the challenge: 15 s) and say: "Can't do jumping jacks? Breathe instead."
- The "Take 5?" nudge comes ~35 s after the session starts. If you spend longer than that on steps 3–6,
  it will already be waiting when you look back at the watch: that is fine, just tap End.
- Save as `pitch_assets/demo_fallback.mp4` (H.264, 1080p, under 100 MB), then tell the AI to check it.
