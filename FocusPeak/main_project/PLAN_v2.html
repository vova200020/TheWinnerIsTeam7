    FocusPeak: build plan for approval (v2)

    📌 Key points

    • Product in one sentence: a Fenix 7 Pro app that scores each focus session, makes you earn each phone break with your heart rate, and learns when and where you focus best.
    • Problem statement fit: the struggle is focus. The app uses 6 watch sensors for real work: heart rate, accelerometer, Global Positioning System (GPS), barometer, temperature and Pulse Ox.
    • Each design choice has a published source (section 4). Two of your claims have no source that I found: the 90-minute limit and the pressure-to-oxygen link. The plan keeps 90 minutes as a design cap only.
    • Session timing: start at 24 min focus and 6 min break, the tested protocol. The block grows with your own scores up to 90 min, and the break grows with it up to 15 min.
    • Sleep and readiness: the watch API gives no sleep score and no measured sleep times. The iPhone can read the sleep of last night from Apple Health, where Garmin Connect writes it. That path is legal, local and good for the privacy message.
    • Two programs: the watch app (I build it) and the iPhone app (the team builds it in Xcode). Section 8 gives the message contract between them.
    • Privacy is a selling point: no account, no cloud. Data stays on the wrist and on the phone of the user.
    • Versions: V0 skeleton (15 min), V1 focus loop (45 min), V2 personal baseline (30 min), V3 phone link (30 min). Code freeze at 3:15 pm.

    Strava records a run, scores it and shows your trend. FocusPeak does the same for a focus session.

    🔬 4. The science behind each choice

    Seven design choices, each with its source. The effects are small to medium: the pitch must not promise more.

    Design choiceWhat the source foundSourceStatus
    Move before the breakOne bout of exercise improves cognition: mean effect 0.33, attention 0.37, executive function 0.36 (standardized mean difference)Meta-review of 30 meta-analyses, 2025, PubMed 39883421✅ Supported
    The app decides when the break comes87 students. Fixed breaks (24 min + 6 min, or 12 + 3) gave less fatigue and more concentration than self-chosen breaks, with the same work done in less timeBiwer 2023, British Journal of Educational Psychology✅ Supported
    Breaks restore energy. Longer breaks after hard work22 samples, 2,335 people. Short breaks raise vigor (d = 0.36) and lower fatigue (d = 0.35). The performance gain grows with break length. Hard tasks can need more than 10 minAlbulescu 2022, PLOS ONE✅ Supported
    Phone out of reach during focusAttention test scores were lower with a phone on the desk. A 2023 meta-analysis found a significant negative effect, but some replications found noneSkowronek 2023, Scientific Reports⚠️ Supported, mixed
    Sleep in the readiness bar61 studies. Short sleep lowers sustained attention (g = -0.41) and executive function (g = -0.32)Lowe 2017, Neuroscience and Biobehavioral Reviews (from a search summary, page not opened)✅ Supported
    Your own best hour of dayPerformance is better when the test time matches the chronotype of the person. Results differ by task typeJankowski 2023, Journal of Intelligence⚠️ Supported, mixed
    Temperature in the readiness barOffice performance rises up to 21 to 22 °C and falls above 23 to 24 °CSeppänen 2006, Indoor Air⚠️ Older source
    90-minute focus limitNo source found today. Attention on monotone tasks starts to fall after minutes, not after 90 minNone❌ Design cap only
    Pressure and Pulse Ox as focus signalsNo source foundNone❌ Not claimed
    ⚠️ LimitI read the search summaries of these papers, not the full texts. The journals are in the top quartile of their fields to my knowledge, but I did not check the quartile lists today. Open each link before a number goes on a slide.
    💡 Key ideaThe science gives the start recipe (24 + 6). It also shows that people differ, and that is the reason for the personal baseline.

    4.1 Session timing that follows from the sources

    ItemValueReason
    Start recipe24 min focus, 6 min breakThe tested protocol of Biwer 2023
    Break ruleOne quarter of the block, at least 5 min, at most 15 minSame ratio as the tested protocol. Longer breaks after longer work (Albulescu 2022)
    Block growthPlus 5 min after 3 sessions in a row with a Focus Score of 70 or more. Minus 5 min after 2 sessions below 50The within-person rule: your data moves your recipe
    Largest block90 min, then a 15 min breakYour proposal, kept as a safety cap
    Break content30 to 60 s of movement to open the gate, then free time with the phoneExercise effect (meta-review 2025). The rest of the break is recovery

    🧠 7. The personal baseline (the innovation)

    The app starts with the 24 + 6 recipe. In the first 14 days it collects your sessions, and then it replaces the recipe with your own numbers.

    What it learnsHow (all on the watch)What the user sees
    Your best hoursMean Focus Score in 4 day parts: morning, afternoon, evening, night"Your best time: morning (score 82)"
    Your best placesGPS fix matched to at most 5 saved places within 150 m. The user picks the label from a list"Library 84, Home 61"
    Your block lengthThe growth rule of section 4.1, between 15 and 90 min"Your block: 35 min + 9 min break"
    Your sleep effectMean Focus Score after short nights and after long nights"After a night under 6 h your score is 14 points lower"
    Your usual valuesRunning mean of start heart rate and temperatureInputs to the readiness bar
    📘 PrivacyThe session log and the places stay in the storage of the watch. In V1 and V2 no data leaves the watch. In V3 the watch talks only to the iPhone of the user. No account and no cloud: say this in the pitch.

    🏗️ 8. Architecture and the iPhone app

    8.1 Message contract between the watch and the iPhone

    The Xcode team can start now with this table. Each message is one small dictionary.

    DirectionMessageThe receiver does this
    Watch to phone{"t":"state","s":"focus","left_s":1440}Show LOCKED and the time left
    Watch to phone{"t":"state","s":"earn","hr":96,"target":104}Show "Move to unlock" with the live heart rate
    Watch to phone{"t":"state","s":"break","left_s":360}Show UNLOCKED and the countdown
    Watch to phone{"t":"summary","score":82,"min":24,"place":"Library","streak":5}Add a card to the session feed
    Phone to watch{"t":"sleep","hours":6.4}Use it in the readiness bar and the baseline
