Why it works

Fixed breaks beat self-chosen breaks. Less fatigue, more concentration, same work done. Biwer 2023
Move before the break. One bout of exercise gives a small to medium gain in attention. Meta-review 2025
Longer breaks after hard work. Short breaks raise energy, and hard tasks can need more than 10 min. Albulescu 2022
Phone out of reach. Attention is lower with a phone on the desk. Skowronek 2023
The Focus Score is a proxy: time on task, wrist stillness and a calm heart rate.
