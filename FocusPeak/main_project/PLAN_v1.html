    FocusPeak: build plan for approval (v1)

    📌 Key points

    • Product in one sentence: a Fenix 7 Pro app that scores each focus session, makes you earn each phone break with your heart rate, and learns when and where you focus best.
    • Problem statement fit: the struggle is focus. The app uses 6 watch sensors for real work: heart rate, accelerometer, Global Positioning System (GPS), barometer, temperature and Pulse Ox.
    • The plan cuts scope hard. One watch app is the product. The phone page is version 3. The watch face and the community are a slide, not code.
    • Three of your ideas do not work as written: the app cannot set do-not-disturb, cannot lock a phone, and barometric pressure is not a focus signal. Section 2 gives the replacement for each.
    • Four versions, each one a demo that works: V0 skeleton (15 min), V1 focus loop (45 min), V2 personal baseline (30 min), V3 phone gate (30 min, optional). Code freeze at 3:15 pm.
    • I need 5 approvals (section 8). After that I can work without you, except for steps on the real watch.

    🧭 1. The product

    Strava records a run, scores it and shows your trend. FocusPeak does the same for a focus session.

    Table 1. The plan against the five judging criteria
    CriterionHow this plan scores
    Problem-Solution Fit"You are the user": focus is on the organizers' own list. The phone is the named enemy, and the break gate deals with it
    Execution and DeploymentA sideloaded app that a judge can run on the watch. Demo mode makes each screen reachable in 60 s
    User Experience and DesignOne number per screen. A calm session screen. Buttons only, touch is a bonus
    Impact and ValueHabit loop: prompt (break alert), action (30 s of movement), reward (phone time, score, streak)
    Creativity and Approach"Earn your scroll" with heart rate, and advice that comes from your own data, not from a fixed recipe

    Each row is checked against the Connect IQ documents of software development kit (SDK) 9.1.0 or a published study.

    Your ideaVerdictWhyWhat the plan does instead

    Lock the phone⚠️ Soft lock onlyA Connect IQ app cannot control the phone. A real block needs a native phone app with special permissions: not a 2-hour taskV1: honor system, the watch sees and scores restlessness. V3: a phone page that shows LOCKED until the heart rate gate opens
    Barometer: more pressure gives more oxygen and better focus❌ Weak claimWeather moves pressure by a few percent. That does not change blood oxygen in a healthy person.     Pulse Ox as a focus input⚠️ Small roleA wrist reading needs a still arm and is close to constant in healthy usersOne spot reading on the readiness screen. It lowers readiness only below 92 %
    Temperature⚠️ With a labelOffice work performance peaks near 21 to 22 °C (Seppänen 2006). But the watch sensor reads warm because it touches the skinUse the change against your own usual value, not the absolute number
    Readiness score and sleep data from Garmin❌ No APISDK 9.1.0 has no call for sleep score, training readiness or heart rate variability statusBody Battery, stress, resting heart rate, recovery time and hours since wake time: all available

    Community, leaderboards, shared placesCut to a slideForest and Opal already have friends and leaderboards. It is not the new part, and it needs a serverA streak on the watch. One roadmap slide. Home is never shared because nothing leaves the watch
    Workout to unlock the phone✅ Keep, it is the hookShort exercise gives a small to medium gain in attention (standardized mean difference 0.37, meta-review of 30 meta-analyses, 2025)30 to 60 s of movement. A "Skip" path always exists, and it costs the streak
    Within-person changes✅ Keep, it is the innovationNo focus app found in the search adapts to the body data of the userSection 5
    ⚠️ LimitThe watch cannot measure focus directly. The Focus Score is a proxy from time on task, wrist stillness and a calm heart rate. The pitch must say "proxy".

    🧠 5. The personal baseline (the innovation)

    The app starts with a standard recipe and replaces it with your own numbers, session by session.

    What it learnsHow (all on the watch)What the user sees
    Your best hoursMean Focus Score in 4 day parts: morning, afternoon, evening, night"Your best time: morning (score 82)"
    Your best placesGPS fix matched to at most 5 saved places within 150 m. The user picks the label from a list"Library 84, Home 61"
    Your block lengthTypical length of the sessions that scored 70 or more, kept between 15 and 50 min"Suggested block: 35 min"
    Your usual valuesRunning mean of start heart rate and temperatureInputs to the readiness bar
    📘 PrivacyThe session log and the places stay in the storage of the watch. In V1 and V2 no data leaves the watch. In V3 only two values leave it: locked or unlocked, and seconds left.
